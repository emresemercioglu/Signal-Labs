Living competitor pages

A page for every competitor that maintains itself

Point CIx at a competitor and it builds the whole picture, then rewrites it as the competitor changes.

  • Offerings, positioning, pricing and SWOT on one page, not eleven tabs of research.
  • Head-to-head scoring across product, usability, performance, brand, support and momentum.
  • A market positioning map that stays current instead of being redrawn in a slide.
  • Full version history: see what changed, when, and roll it back.
Signals & actions

Every competitor move, scored and turned into work

Websites, pricing, launches, funding, hiring and news are checked on your plan's schedule: daily on Free, every 4 hours on Pro, hourly on Ultra.

  • Scored 1–10 so a pricing change outranks a blog post.
  • Drafted for you: a comparison page, battlecard edit, website snippet or email.
  • Approve, edit or discard, and pages and battlecards update with it.
Battlecards

Battlecards in minutes, written for whoever reads them

Built from live intelligence rather than last quarter's research.

  • Tailored to the team: sales, marketing, product or leadership.
  • Landscape cards compare several competitors in one view.
  • Objection handling, discovery questions and win strategies, refined in plain language.
Battle Rooms

Work live deals together, with @CIx in the thread

One room per deal, with the client, the competitors involved and everyone who can help win it.

  • Ask @CIx for a counter-position and get talk tracks, not a document link.
  • Mention teammates and they're notified. Deliverables land in the room.
  • Close as won or lost with a note, so the reasoning stays with the deal.
