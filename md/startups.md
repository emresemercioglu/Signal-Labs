## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competitive intelligence for startups

### At what stage should startups invest in competitive intelligence?

From day one. Early positioning and product decisions create path dependencies. Better to establish competitive intelligence early than correct poor positioning later. CIx is affordable and fast enough for pre-seed and seed stage.

### Can one person manage CIx for entire startup?

Yes. Many startups have founder, marketer, or growth lead manage CIx part-time. Self-service nature means entire team can access intelligence without heavy management.

### What if we're pivoting or still finding product-market fit?

CIx is flexible for pivots. Change competitors, update positioning, adjust focus as your strategy evolves. No long-term commitments or rigid enterprise processes.

### Can CIx scale as we grow to enterprise?

Yes. Start free with one competitor, then move to a Team plan scoped to the competitors you track and the seats you need. It's the same platform either way — nothing to migrate, and the competitor pages you already built keep updating.
