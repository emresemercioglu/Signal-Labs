## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competitive intelligence for healthcare and pharmaceuticals

### How does CIx track competitor clinical trials?

CIx monitors clinical trial registries, published results, conference presentations, and regulatory filings. We track competitor trial phases, endpoints, patient populations, and outcomes to provide comprehensive clinical development intelligence.

### Can CIx track competitive regulatory approvals and pathways?

Yes. CIx tracks FDA, EMA, and other regulatory submissions, approvals, and rejections. We monitor competitive regulatory strategies including breakthrough therapy designations, accelerated approvals, and orphan drug status.

### How does CIx help with patent landscape analysis?

CIx monitors competitor patents, patent expirations, patent litigation, and IP strategies. This intelligence is critical for understanding competitive threats from biosimilars, generics, and patent cliff timing.

### Does CIx track reimbursement and payer coverage decisions?

Yes. CIx tracks competitive reimbursement status, payer coverage decisions, formulary placements, and health technology assessments (HTAs). This commercial intelligence is critical for understanding competitive market access strategies.
