## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competitive intelligence for law firms

### How does CIx help law firms with client pitch intelligence?

CIx provides comprehensive intelligence on incumbent firms including recent case wins, practice area strengths, attorney credentials, and client relationships. This helps you understand who you're competing against and how to position your firm effectively in client pitches.

### Can CIx track lateral hire opportunities at competitor firms?

Yes. CIx monitors partner departures, practice group changes, firm restructuring, and other signals of instability at competitor firms. This intelligence helps identify potential lateral hire opportunities and competitive weaknesses.

### How does CIx support legal RFP responses?

CIx provides intelligence on likely competing bidders, their strengths and weaknesses, recent RFP wins/losses, and pricing strategies. This helps you differentiate your RFP response and position competitively.

### Does CIx track legal directory rankings and firm reputation?

Yes. CIx monitors Chambers, Legal 500, Best Lawyers, and other directory rankings, plus attorney recognition and firm reputation trends. This helps you understand competitive positioning in the market.
