## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competitive intelligence for deep tech companies

### How does CIx track technical differentiation in deep tech?

CIx monitors technical publications, research papers, conference presentations, technical blog posts, and patent filings to understand competitive technical approaches. We help deep tech companies validate their technical differentiation and understand competitive technical capabilities.

### Can CIx track competitor patents and IP strategies?

Yes. CIx monitors patent filings, patent grants, and patent landscapes. We track competitive IP strategies, patent portfolio evolution, and patent citation patterns to help deep tech companies understand the patent landscape and competitive IP positioning.

### How does CIx help with long deep tech sales cycles?

Deep tech sales cycles often span 12-24 months. CIx provides continuous competitive intelligence throughout the sales cycle, alerting you when competitors announce relevant developments, funding rounds, technical breakthroughs, or strategic changes that could impact your active deals.

### Does CIx track competitive R&D and funding intelligence?

Yes. CIx tracks competitor funding rounds, R&D investments, research partnerships, technical hires, and strategic R&D directions. This intelligence helps deep tech companies understand competitive technical trajectories and financial positioning.
