## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competitive intelligence for ecommerce & retail

### How does CIx track competitor pricing in real-time?

CIx monitors competitor pricing across their websites, marketplaces, and sales channels. We track price changes, promotional pricing, and dynamic pricing strategies in real-time, alerting you immediately when competitors adjust their prices.

### Can CIx track competitor activity on Amazon and other marketplaces?

Yes. CIx monitors competitor presence, pricing, reviews, and rankings on Amazon, eBay, and other marketplaces. Track marketplace performance, seller strategies, and customer sentiment across all major ecommerce platforms.

### How does CIx help track competitor promotional strategies?

CIx tracks competitor sales events, discount campaigns, seasonal promotions, and coupon codes. We help you understand competitive promotional calendars, discount depths, and promotional frequency to optimize your own promotional strategy.

### Can CIx monitor competitor product catalog changes?

Yes. CIx tracks when competitors add, remove, or update products in their catalogs. Monitor SKU additions, product discontinuations, and inventory availability to identify catalog gaps and product opportunities.
