## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competitive intelligence for SaaS companies

### How does CIx track SaaS competitor features?

CIx monitors product pages, release notes, changelogs, Product Hunt launches, beta programs, and user reviews. We provide continuous feature tracking for fast-moving SaaS products, so you're never surprised by a competitor launch.

### Can CIx track PLG metrics like free-to-paid conversion?

We track observable PLG strategies including free trial terms, freemium models, and upgrade flows. While actual conversion metrics aren't public, we infer competitive PLG effectiveness from pricing pages, public statements, user reviews, and growth patterns.

### How does CIx help identify product gaps vs competitors?

CIx continuously tracks competitor feature releases and product updates. We show you side-by-side feature comparisons, highlight what's new, and identify gaps in your product vs competitors. Know exactly what buyers will compare and what objections to expect.

### Can CIx track competitor integrations and partnerships?

Yes. CIx monitors integration marketplaces, partnership announcements, API documentation updates, and app store listings. Integration ecosystem is a key competitive moat in SaaS—we help you track what integrations competitors are building and prioritize yours accordingly.
