## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competitive intelligence for defense contractors

### How does CIx track defense contract bid intelligence?

CIx monitors contract announcements, industry publications, press releases, and public bid data. We help defense contractors understand the competitive landscape for upcoming bids, including likely competitors, teaming arrangements, and competitive capabilities.

### Can CIx track competitor teaming arrangements and partnerships?

Yes. CIx monitors partnership announcements, teaming agreements, subcontractor relationships, and strategic alliances. We help defense contractors understand competitive teaming strategies and identify potential partnership opportunities or threats.

### How does CIx help with capability comparison in defense?

CIx tracks competitive technical capabilities, past performance, facility certifications, and clearance levels. We help defense contractors understand how their capabilities compare to competitors and identify areas for differentiation in bids.

### Does CIx track regulatory compliance and certifications?

Yes. CIx monitors competitive certifications, clearance levels, facility security clearances, and regulatory compliance positioning. We help defense contractors understand competitive regulatory positioning and identify certification gaps.
