## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competitive intelligence for gaming companies

### How does CIx track competitive game updates and content?

CIx monitors game patch notes, update announcements, content releases, seasonal events, and live-service changes. We track competitive games across mobile, PC, and console platforms to give you real-time intelligence on what competitors are shipping.

### Can CIx track competitor monetization strategies in gaming?

Yes. CIx tracks competitive monetization approaches including in-game purchases, battle pass pricing, subscription models, gacha mechanics, and promotional pricing. We help gaming companies understand competitive monetization strategies and pricing experiments.

### How does CIx help monitor player sentiment and reviews?

CIx monitors player reviews on app stores, Steam, Reddit, Discord, and social media. We track player sentiment, community feedback, and competitive game reception to help you understand what players love and hate about competing games.

### Does CIx track influencer and streamer competitive strategies?

Yes. CIx monitors competitive influencer partnerships, streamer campaigns, sponsored content, and content creator collaborations. We help gaming companies understand which influencers competitors work with and how they structure these partnerships.
