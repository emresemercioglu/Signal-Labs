## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is CIx different from traditional CI tools?

+

CIx is built for real-time competitive intelligence, not quarterly updates. We automate routine monitoring, generate AI-powered battlecards in seconds, and embed CI directly into your workflows (Salesforce, Slack, Gong). Traditional CI tools require manual updates and exist as separate platforms - CIx meets your teams where they work.

### Do I need a dedicated CI team to use CIx?

+

No. CIx is designed for both dedicated CI teams and companies without CI resources. Solo founders, lean startups, and individual contributors can use CIx effectively. For enterprises with CI teams, CIx amplifies their impact by automating routine work.

### Which solution is right for me?

+

Start with your role or team. If you're in sales, explore [/solutions/sales](https://usesignallabs.com/solutions/sales). If you're solving a specific problem (like sales enablement), explore [/use-cases/sales-enablement](https://usesignallabs.com/use-cases/sales-enablement). If your industry has unique needs (like legal or healthcare), explore industry-specific solutions. All solutions are powered by the same CIx platform.

### Can I customize CIx for my company?

+

Yes. CIx is fully customizable. You can add your own playbooks, integrate internal data sources (call transcripts, sales notes, docs), tailor battlecard templates, and configure intelligence for your brand's tone and perspective.

### How long does implementation take?

+

Most teams are up and running the same day. Add competitors, generate battlecards, and start enabling teams within hours. Enterprise implementations with custom integrations typically take 1-2 weeks.

### What integrations does CIx support?

+

CIx integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Gong, Chorus, Highspot, Seismic, Guru, and more. Enterprise customers can build custom integrations via API.
