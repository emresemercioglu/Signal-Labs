## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is CIx different from traditional market research for due diligence?

+

Traditional market research provides point-in-time competitive analysis. CIx provides continuous intelligence: pre-investment for due diligence, post-investment for portfolio monitoring. Instead of one-time reports, you get ongoing competitive intelligence across all portfolio companies.

### Can CIx replace full due diligence?

+

No. CIx accelerates and enhances the competitive intelligence component of due diligence. You still need financial diligence, technical diligence, reference checks, and other due diligence streams. CIx makes competitive diligence faster, more comprehensive, and more systematic.

### How long does competitive due diligence take with CIx?

+

Comprehensive competitive landscape analysis: hours instead of weeks. Most investors complete competitive due diligence component in 1-2 days with CIx versus 1-2 weeks manually.

### Can we share CIx with portfolio companies?

+

Yes. Many VC firms provide CIx access to portfolio companies as value-add. Portfolio companies use CIx for their competitive intelligence, firm uses CIx for portfolio monitoring.

### How does CIx help with market mapping for thesis development?

+

CIx maps competitive landscapes across entire markets or sectors. Use for thesis development, sector analysis, and sourcing strategy. Understand competitive dynamics before diving into specific deals.

### Can CIx identify red flags in competitive claims?

+

Yes. CIx validates competitive claims against objective competitive data. Identify when "no real competitors" isn't accurate, when "2 years ahead" is overstated, or when moat claims don't hold up.

### What about private/stealth competitors?

+

CIx primarily tracks public competitive signals. Stealth competitors with no public presence are difficult to discover. However, CIx often identifies stealth companies through funding announcements, hiring patterns, and industry signals.

### Can we customize CIx for our investment focus?

+

Yes. Configure CIx to focus on your investment sectors, stage preferences, and competitive criteria. Customize for your firm's diligence framework.
