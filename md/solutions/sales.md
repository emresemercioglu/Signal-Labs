## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is CIx different from Crayon or Klue?

+

CIx is built for real-time competitive intelligence with AI at its core. While traditional CI tools require manual content creation and updates, CIx automates battlecard generation, monitors competitors 24/7, and updates intelligence automatically. We also embed directly into your workflow (Salesforce, Slack, Gong) rather than being a separate platform. Traditional tools are designed for CI teams to create content. CIx is designed for every team to access intelligence.

### How long does it take to get our sales team using CIx?

+

Most sales teams are up and running the same day. Add your competitors (or use AI discovery), generate battlecards, integrate with Salesforce/Slack, and start enabling your team within hours. No lengthy implementations or training required.

### Can CIx integrate with our existing sales tools?

+

Yes. CIx integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Gong, Chorus, and major sales enablement platforms (Highspot, Seismic, Guru). We also offer a [REST API, native SDKs, and MCP server](https://usesignallabs.com/docs) for custom integrations and AI-powered workflows. We meet your team where they already work, embedding competitive intelligence directly into their daily workflow.

### Where does CIx get its competitive data?

+

CIx monitors public sources (competitor websites, product updates, pricing pages, press releases, reviews, social media) and can also integrate your internal data sources (sales calls, win/loss interviews, internal docs, CRM data). You control what intelligence is tracked and how it's organized.

### How much does CIx cost for sales teams?

+

CIx offers flexible pricing based on team size and features. Most sales teams start with our Team plan, which includes automated battlecards, real-time monitoring, and core integrations. Enterprise plans include custom integrations, advanced analytics, and dedicated support. [Contact us](https://usesignallabs.com/solutions/../contact) for detailed pricing.

### Do we need a dedicated CI team to use CIx?

+

No. CIx is designed for sales teams to use directly without requiring a CI team. However, if you have a CI team, CIx amplifies their impact by automating research and distribution, letting them focus on strategy instead of manual updates.

### How accurate is the AI-generated content?

+

CIx uses source-verified intelligence with citations for every claim. You can review sources, edit content, and add internal context. Think of CIx as an intelligent research assistant that drafts content 80-90% complete, which you can refine and approve before distribution.

### Can we customize battlecards for our brand?

+

Yes. CIx supports custom battlecard templates, brand guidelines, tone of voice, and role-specific formats. You can create different battlecard types for SDRs, AEs, leadership, or customer success teams - all auto-generated from the same intelligence.
