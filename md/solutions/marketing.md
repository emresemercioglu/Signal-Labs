## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is CIx different from social listening or brand monitoring tools?

+

Social listening tools track brand mentions and social sentiment. CIx tracks competitive positioning, messaging, campaigns, and product intelligence across all channels. We focus on actionable competitive intelligence for marketing strategy, not just social monitoring. While social listening tells you what people are saying, CIx tells you what competitors are doing, how they're positioning, where differentiation opportunities exist, and recommended actions to take.

### Can CIx track competitors across different channels?

+

Yes. CIx tracks competitors across multiple channels including websites, social media, news, forums, user review platforms, and more. Our team works with you to configure additional channel monitoring based on where your competitors are most active including Google Ads, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, Discord, and other relevant channels.

### How real-time is the intelligence?

+

CIx monitors competitors 24/7. When competitors announce a new feature, update their messaging, or make any public announcement, you get alerts within hours. Intelligence is always current.

### Can CIx help with positioning for new product launches?

+

Yes. CIx provides comprehensive competitive landscape analysis for launch positioning. Understand competitive positioning, identify white space, and craft differentiated positioning before launch.

### Does CIx integrate with marketing tools like HubSpot or Marketo?

+

Yes. CIx can integrate with your marketing platforms including HubSpot, Marketo, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, and more. For custom workflows, you can also use our [REST API, SDKs, and MCP server](https://usesignallabs.com/docs) to bring competitive intelligence into any tool.
