## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is CIx different from product analytics or user research tools?

+

Product analytics tools tell you how your users behave. User research tools tell you what your users want. CIx tells you what competitors are building, launching, and positioning. We focus on competitive product intelligence: feature comparisons, roadmap signals, technical approaches, UI/UX analysis, and differentiation opportunities. While analytics and research inform internal decisions, CIx provides external competitive context for product strategy.

### Does CIx integrate with product management tools like Jira, Productboard, or Aha?

+

Yes. CIx can integrate with your product management stack including Jira, Productboard, Aha, Linear, and more. You can also use our [REST API and SDKs](https://usesignallabs.com/docs) to pull competitive intelligence directly into your roadmap planning tools and feature prioritization workflows.

### How real-time is the intelligence?

+

CIx monitors competitors 24/7. When competitors announce a new feature, update their product, release technical documentation, or make product-related announcements, you get alerts within hours. Product intelligence is always current.

### Can CIx track competitor UI/UX and design patterns?

+

Yes. Beyond feature tracking, CIx analyzes competitive user experiences, interface design, interaction patterns, and usability approaches. Benchmark your product experience against competitors and identify UX differentiation opportunities.

### How does CIx help with build vs. buy decisions?

+

CIx tracks how competitors approach capabilities: internal builds, vendor solutions, open-source implementations, or hybrid approaches. Understand market technical trends, vendor adoption patterns, and competitive architecture decisions to inform your own build vs. buy strategy.
