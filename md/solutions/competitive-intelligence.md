## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will CIx replace our CI team?

+

No. CIx amplifies CI teams by automating routine work (data collection, battlecard formatting, repetitive questions) so CI professionals focus on strategic work (pattern detection, strategic implications, competitive strategy). CIx is a CI productivity multiplier, not a CI replacement. The most successful CI teams use CIx to scale their impact 10x.

### How is CIx different from traditional CI tools like Crayon or Klue?

+

Traditional CI tools require manual content creation and curation. CI teams manually input competitive information, create battlecards, and update content. CIx automates this: AI-generated battlecards, automatic monitoring, self-updating intelligence. Traditional tools are for CI teams to create content. CIx is for CI teams to scale intelligence organization-wide.

### Can we integrate our existing competitive intelligence and research?

+

Yes. Upload existing battlecards, research reports, competitive frameworks, and proprietary intelligence. CIx incorporates your existing IP while adding automated monitoring and AI-powered updates.

### How do we maintain quality control with AI-generated content?

+

CIx provides review and approval workflows. You control what gets published, how it's branded, and what messaging is approved. AI accelerates creation, you ensure quality. Think "AI-assisted" not "AI-autonomous."

### Can multiple CI team members collaborate in CIx?

+

Yes. CIx supports team collaboration: shared workspaces, task assignments, review workflows, commenting, version control. Built for CI teams, not just individuals.

### Can we measure the ROI of CIx for our CI function?

+

Yes. Track time saved (hours per week), stakeholders supported (before vs. after), battlecard production (quantity and recency), and intelligence attribution (impact on wins). Most CI teams see positive ROI within first month.
