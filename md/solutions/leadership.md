## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is CIx different from traditional market research or consulting?

+

Traditional market research provides point-in-time analysis. CIx provides continuous, real-time competitive intelligence. Instead of quarterly reports, you get daily intelligence. Instead of retrospective analysis, you get forward-looking insights. CIx is a platform for continuous intelligence, not a one-time research project.

### Do we need a dedicated CI team to use CIx for strategic intelligence?

+

No. CIx is designed for executives and strategy teams to access intelligence directly. While CI teams can configure and curate intelligence, leadership can access strategic insights without intermediaries.

### How current is the strategic intelligence?

+

CIx monitors competitive landscape 24/7. Strategic moves like funding rounds, acquisitions, market expansions, and major announcements are tracked in real-time. You get alerts within hours of competitive strategic moves.

### Can CIx support M&A due diligence?

+

Yes. CIx provides competitive intelligence for M&A target evaluation: competitive positioning, market landscape, competitive threats to target, and strategic rationale. While not replacing full due diligence, CIx accelerates competitive analysis component.

### Is this too tactical for board presentations?

+

No. CIx provides strategic intelligence appropriate for board level: executive summaries, key trends, strategic implications, and recommended actions. You control the detail level - tactical for operators, strategic for board.

### Can we track emerging competitors before they're significant threats?

+

Yes. CIx monitors new market entrants, funding announcements, and emerging competitors. Early warning system helps you track potential threats before they impact your business.
