## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about enterprise competitive intelligence

### What's included in Enterprise plan?

Custom seats and usage limits, your full competitive set tracked continuously, internal data integration, workflow embedding, custom integrations via our API and MCP server, 24h SLA support, a dedicated CSM, bi-weekly strategy calls and team workshops. Security and deployment requirements such as SSO, SAML or a dedicated environment are scoped on the call — tell us what your security review needs and we'll tell you where we are on it.

### How long does enterprise implementation take?

Typically 2-4 weeks for full implementation including integrations, internal data connection, team onboarding, and workflow embedding. Faster for simpler deployments.

### Can we integrate with our custom internal tools?

Yes. Enterprise customers get full API access for custom integrations. Our [REST API](https://usesignallabs.com/docs), JavaScript and Python SDKs, and [MCP server](https://usesignallabs.com/docs/mcp) let you integrate competitive intelligence into any internal tool, Slack bot, CRM workflow, or AI agent. We also support custom connectors for unique enterprise tools. Read the [API launch announcement](https://usesignallabs.com/blog/introducing-signal-labs-api-mcp-server) for more details.

### How does CIx handle security and data residency?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is scoped by role within your organisation, and each organisation's intelligence is isolated from every other tenant. If your procurement process requires specific certifications, audit logging or a particular data residency arrangement, bring it to the call - we will tell you plainly what is in place today and what we would need to build for you.
