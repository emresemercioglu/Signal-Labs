## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is CIx different from traditional enablement platforms?

+

Enablement platforms (Highspot, Seismic, Guru) are content management and delivery systems. CIx is competitive intelligence creation and automation. CIx generates and maintains competitive content that you publish to enablement platforms. They're complementary: CIx creates intelligence, enablement platforms deliver it.

### Can we integrate CIx with our existing enablement tools?

+

Yes. CIx integrates with Highspot, Seismic, Guru, Showpad, Mindtickle, and more. Competitive intelligence automatically synced to your enablement platform. No manual uploads or duplicate systems.

### How do we maintain battlecard quality with AI generation?

+

CIx provides review and approval workflows. AI generates drafts, you review and refine. Think "AI-assisted" not "AI-autonomous." Most enablement teams review and approve in 5-10 minutes versus 20-40 hours of manual creation.

### What if our battlecard template is highly customized?

+

Upload your custom templates. CIx generates battlecards matching your format, structure, and branding. Your templates, our automation.

### Can we track which reps are using battlecards?

+

Yes. CIx tracks battlecard access by rep, by deal, by competitor. See adoption across team, identify power users and those who need support.

### What's the time-to-value for sales enablement with CIx?

+

Most teams generate their first battlecards within hours of setup. Full competitive enablement (10+ competitors with multi-focus battlecards) within first week. Immediate value.
