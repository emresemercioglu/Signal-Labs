## Frequently Asked Questions

### What competitor changes does CIx monitor?

+

CIx watches each competitor's website and product pages, pricing, feature and product launches, funding and acquisitions, hiring activity, press and public content. Every change it finds becomes a signal, scored 1-10 for importance and linked back to the exact page or article it came from.

### How quickly do we hear about a competitor move?

+

Continuously. CIx re-checks each competitor on its own schedule rather than waiting for someone to run a report, so new signals appear in the feed as they are detected and the affected sections of that competitor's page rewrite themselves. Nobody on your team has to refresh anything.

### How do we know which changes actually matter?

+

Every signal carries an importance score, so a repositioned homepage headline doesn't read the same as a new pricing tier. The moves that matter also arrive with a recommended action - update this battlecard, revise this comparison page, send this competitive email - waiting in your actions inbox, so monitoring turns into work that ships.

### Can we trust what CIx tells us about a competitor?

+

Yes, and you can check it. Every claim on a competitor page and every signal in the feed links to the source it was taken from, so a rep can verify a pricing or feature claim before repeating it to a customer instead of trusting an unattributed summary.

### How do we know which competitors we should be monitoring?

+

CIx suggests them. It analyses your website and category and proposes competitors at both the company and the product level, with its reasoning for each one, so you catch the product-level rivals that don't show up in a company-level list. Add the ones that matter and CIx builds a living page for each.

### How many competitors can we monitor?

+

The free plan tracks one competitor with real-time signals and a monthly battlecard, with no credit card and no expiry. Team plans are scoped to the number of competitors you track and the number of people who need seats - talk to us and we'll quote it on the call.
