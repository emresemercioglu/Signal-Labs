## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does CIx identify positioning white space?

+

CIx analyzes competitive messaging across websites, marketing materials, and sales collateral to map where competitors position themselves. AI-powered analysis identifies crowded positioning areas and unoccupied white space where you can differentiate uniquely.

### Can CIx track when competitors change their positioning?

+

Yes. CIx monitors competitive positioning in real-time, alerting you when competitors shift messaging, launch campaigns, or reposition products. This allows you to adjust your positioning proactively rather than react months later.

### How does positioning intelligence differ from market research?

+

Traditional market research is point-in-time and takes months. CIx provides continuous, real-time competitive positioning intelligence. Market research tells you what buyers think. CIx shows you what competitors claim and where positioning gaps exist right now.

### Who should own positioning intelligence in our organization?

+

Typically Product Marketing or Competitive Intelligence teams. However, positioning intelligence benefits Product (roadmap prioritization), Marketing (campaign messaging), Sales (differentiation talking points), and Leadership (strategic positioning decisions).

### How often should we update our positioning based on intelligence?

+

Monitor positioning intelligence continuously, but update your positioning strategically (typically quarterly). Major positioning shifts require cross-functional alignment and messaging updates across all channels. Use intelligence to make informed decisions, not reactive changes.

### Can we track positioning effectiveness over time?

+

Yes. CIx tracks positioning evolution and measures differentiation impact. See how your positioning compares to competitors over time, track white space opportunities as they open or close, and measure whether your positioning changes improve market perception and competitive win rates.
