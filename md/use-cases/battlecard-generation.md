## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to generate a battlecard?

+

60 seconds for AI generation. Then 5-10 minutes for review and refinement. Total time from start to publish: 5-10 minutes vs 2-3 weeks for manual battlecard creation. That's 100x faster.

### What's included in a CIx battlecard?

+

Overview & positioning, competitive analysis (their strengths, our advantages), features & capabilities comparison, pricing intelligence, win strategies & talk tracks, and full source attribution. Every claim is sourced and timestamped for transparency.

### What are multi-focus battlecards?

+

Role-specific battlecards for different teams. Sales-focus emphasizes objection handling and win strategies. Marketing-focus emphasizes positioning and messaging. Product-focus emphasizes feature comparison. Leadership-focus emphasizes strategic positioning. Each battlecard addresses unique team needs.

### How do battlecards stay current?

+

Automatic refresh. CIx monitors competitors 24/7. When competitors change pricing, launch features, or update messaging, battlecards automatically refresh with new intelligence. You're notified of updates. No manual maintenance required.

### Can I use my existing battlecard templates?

+

Yes. CIx can generate content matching your existing battlecard format, style, and structure. Upload your template and CIx will generate battlecards that match your established format while maintaining brand consistency.

### Where can I publish battlecards?

+

Publish to CIx platform and automatically sync to Highspot, Seismic, Salesforce, or other enablement platforms. Export to PDF or document format. Distribute automatically to your team when battlecards are created or updated.
