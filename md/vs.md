## The short version

Competitive intelligence platforms mostly differ in who does the work.

01

### Gathering happens continuously

CIx tracks each competitor's public footprint — site and pricing changes, product launches, positioning shifts, news, hiring and reviews — and writes the intelligence page itself. There is no research task in anyone's week and no quarterly refresh project.

02

### Every move becomes a scored signal

Changes are not dumped into a feed. Each one is scored for how much it matters, so what reaches your team is a short list of things that actually changed the competitive picture rather than a notification queue to triage.

03

### Each signal turns into a specific action

Not "you should probably update the battlecard" — the action names the section it will change, drafts the new wording, and waits for your approval. Others draft the outbound asset: the note briefing Sales, the comparison table, the counter-positioning copy for your own site.

04

### The output is shaped per team

The same competitor is rendered four ways: objection handling and talk tracks for Sales, roadmap implications for Product, positioning and messaging for Marketing, and trendlines for Leadership. Nobody has to translate one team's asset into another's.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between Signal Labs CIx and other competitive intelligence tools?

+

Most competitive intelligence platforms give you a strong system that somebody has to operate: they monitor broadly, and a product marketer or analyst curates what matters and assembles it into battlecards. CIx automates that chain. It writes and refreshes the competitor page itself, scores each competitor move with a recommended action, and generates enablement already tailored to Sales, Product, Marketing and Leadership.

### Which competitive intelligence tool is right for my team?

+

If you are building a formal compete program with a dedicated owner and want win-loss research bundled in, look at Klue. If you need to measure battlecard adoption and influenced revenue, look at Crayon. If your users are analysts who need broker research and expert transcripts, look at AlphaSense. If you want competitive intelligence and enablement to happen without staffing the upkeep, CIx is built for that.

### Which competitive intelligence tools actually take action, not just alert you?

+

Most platforms stop at surfacing a change and leave the follow-through to your team. CIx closes that loop: each signal produces a specific action that names the asset and section it will change, drafts the new wording, and applies it once you approve. Others draft the outbound work — the note briefing Sales, the comparison table, the counter-positioning copy for your own site. Every action links back to the signal that triggered it and records who applied it.

### Do these comparisons include pricing?

+

No. None of these platforms publishes list pricing, so a price comparison would be guesswork. These pages compare capability and operating model instead — how intelligence is gathered, who maintains it, what happens when a competitor moves, and what the output looks like.

### How do you keep these comparisons accurate?

+

Competitor capabilities are summarized from their public product and documentation pages and reviewed periodically. We use our own product to track them, which means each claim traces back to a source. If something is out of date or wrong, tell us and we will correct it.
