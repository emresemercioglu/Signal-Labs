## Knowing is not the job. Acting is.

Every competitive intelligence tool can tell you something changed. CIx turns that into a specific, previewable change you can ship.

### It revises your intelligence

-   **Update battlecard strengths** Targets the strengths section of the battlecard and drafts the revision, because the change affects how reps should position against them in live deals.
-   **Refresh revenue and ARR** Replaces an estimate on the competitor page with the figure the new filing or announcement actually reported.
-   **Add a strategic bet** Records a product expansion as a new strategic bet, so the picture reflects where they are heading and not only what they shipped.
-   **Add a market trend** Captures a shift in buyer expectations at the category level when a launch signals more than a one-off feature.

### It drafts what goes out

-   **Brief sales on their launch** An internal note with talking points, so reps can respond in deals that are open right now.
-   **Comparison table versus them** A side-by-side built from current intelligence, ready to drop into a deck or a page.
-   **Counter-positioning copy for your site** A ready-to-paste block that answers their new claim in your own words.
-   **Comparison landing page** A full page positioning you head-to-head against their launch, for paid and organic capture.

Every action links back to the signal that triggered it, carries a priority, and shows a preview before anything changes. You approve it, edit it, or discard it — and CIx records who applied it and when.

## What "automated end to end" actually means

Four steps that normally need a person in the middle. In CIx, the person joins at review.

01

### Gathering happens continuously

CIx tracks each competitor's public footprint — site and pricing changes, product launches, positioning shifts, news, hiring and reviews — and writes the intelligence page itself. There is no research task in anyone's week and no quarterly refresh project.

02

### Every move becomes a scored signal

Changes are not dumped into a feed. Each one is scored for how much it matters, so what reaches your team is a short list of things that actually changed the competitive picture rather than a notification queue to triage.

03

### Each signal turns into a specific action

Not "you should probably update the battlecard" — the action names the section it will change, drafts the new wording, and waits for your approval. Others draft the outbound asset: the note briefing Sales, the comparison table, the counter-positioning copy for your own site.

04

### The output is shaped per team

The same competitor is rendered four ways: objection handling and talk tracks for Sales, roadmap implications for Product, positioning and messaging for Marketing, and trendlines for Leadership. Nobody has to translate one team's asset into another's.

## When Klue is the better choice

We would rather be straight with you than oversell. Klue is the stronger choice if:

-   You want competitive intelligence and win-loss in one program, including human-led buyer and expert interviews.
-   You want an analyst services layer — people running custom research on retainer — bundled with the software rather than bought separately.
-   Deep native delivery inside Salesforce, Highspot or Seismic is a requirement rather than a preference.

One thing worth being clear about: CIx is not only for teams without a competitive intelligence owner. If you have one, they stop spending their week on monitoring and battlecard upkeep and spend it on the judgment calls only they can make — which competitor to press, how to reposition, what the pattern across signals actually means. The difference is what the role does, not whether you need it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best Klue alternative?

+

It depends on whether you want a program or an engine. Klue is the stronger pick if you are building a formal compete function with a dedicated owner and want win-loss research bundled in. Signal Labs CIx is the stronger pick if you want the workflow automated: competitor pages that maintain themselves, every competitor move scored with a recommended action, and battlecards generated per team rather than authored by hand.

### How is Signal Labs CIx different from Klue?

+

The difference is the operating model. Klue gives you a broad platform plus an analyst services layer that a competitive intelligence owner operates. CIx automates that chain end to end — it gathers the intelligence, writes and refreshes the competitor page, scores each move with a recommended action, and generates the enablement tailored to Sales, Product, Marketing and Leadership. Your team reviews and edits instead of researching and assembling.

### Do I need a dedicated competitive intelligence analyst to use CIx?

+

No, and it is equally useful if you have one. The monitoring, page upkeep and battlecard authoring that a compete program normally assigns to a full-time analyst or product marketer are automated, which is what makes CIx workable for lean teams. For teams that do have a dedicated owner, the same automation removes the maintenance half of the job and leaves them on the strategic half — deciding which competitors matter, how to reposition, and what the pattern across signals means.

### Does CIx do win-loss analysis like Klue?

+

Not today. Klue runs a full win-loss program including human-led buyer interviews, and if that is central to your evaluation Klue is the better fit. CIx is focused on competitive intelligence and enablement: tracking competitors, scoring their moves and producing the material your teams use in live deals.

### Can CIx deliver competitive intelligence into Slack and Salesforce?

+

CIx delivers into Slack and email today, and exposes everything through a REST API, JavaScript and Python SDKs and an MCP server, so it can feed CRM dashboards and AI tools programmatically. Klue currently offers deeper native delivery inside Salesforce, Highspot and Seismic. If in-CRM battlecard surfacing is a hard requirement, scope it with us on a call before you decide.

### How long does CIx take to set up?

+

You can add your first competitor and get a living intelligence page with source-linked claims the same day, free, without talking to anyone. There is no implementation project to schedule before you see whether the intelligence is any good.
