The questions teams ask before they switch

What is a competitive intelligence platform?

A competitive intelligence platform collects, analyses and distributes information about the companies you compete with, so sales, product and marketing teams can position against them. Most platforms stop at collection and leave a human analyst to write and share the output. Signal Labs CIx also produces the output: competitor pages that maintain themselves, recommended actions from each competitor move, and battlecards generated on demand.

How does Signal Labs keep competitor information current?

Each competitor page is rebuilt from live sources rather than stored as a static document. CIx re-scrapes the competitor's own site and monitors launches, pricing changes, funding, hiring and news. When something material changes, the page updates and the change is turned into a recommended action for your team. You never schedule a refresh.

How is this different from Klue and Crayon?

Klue and Crayon are collection and curation tools: they gather intel and leave a human to sort it, write it up and distribute it. Signal Labs automates the whole chain from tracking through to enablement. Every competitor gets a page that maintains itself, every meaningful competitor move produces a specific recommended action, and every claim carries a link to the source it came from — so your team reviews and edits the work instead of producing it from scratch.

Can I trust what the AI produces?

Every claim is source-linked. Each statement in a competitor page or battlecard carries a link to the page it was drawn from, so a rep can check the original before repeating it to a buyer. Where CIx does not have confirmation, it says so rather than filling the gap — you can see that in practice in the Battle Room, where it separates confirmed evidence from inference.

How long does it take to get value?

Adding a competitor takes about a minute, and the first living page is generated for you within a few minutes. Battlecards are generated on demand in about the same time it takes to read one. There is no implementation project and no data import.

Do you have an API or MCP server?

Yes. Signal Labs exposes a REST API and a hosted MCP server, so you can pull competitor intelligence into your own tools or query it directly from MCP clients such as Claude and Cursor. Official Python and JavaScript SDKs are available.
